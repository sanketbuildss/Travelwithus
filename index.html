Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful details before planning your Rajasthan trip

Are these prices final?

No. Prices shown are sample demo prices for the design. Confirm real inclusions, dates and taxes before accepting bookings.

Does the booking form send my details?

No. This single-file version is frontend-only. It displays a demo confirmation but does not send or save personal information.

Can I change the destination and travel dates?

Yes. Use the search form near the top. The demo filters the listed tour packages by destination and checks that the check-out date is after check-in.

Can this website accept real bookings later?

Yes, but a secure backend and database would need to be added before using it for real customer bookings or payments.
